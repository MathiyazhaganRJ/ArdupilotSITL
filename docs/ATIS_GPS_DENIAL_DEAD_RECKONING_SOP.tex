\noindent{\large\bfseries\color{aeroblue} 1. Executive Summary \& Threat Taxonomy}

Operating unmanned air vehicles (UAVs) in contested, electronically degraded airspace requires an exact understanding of how ArduPilot's Extended Kalman Filter (EKF3) and Attitude \& Heading Reference System (AHRS) interact with corrupted sensor inputs.

\noindent{\large\bfseries\color{aeroblue} 2. Normal Jammed Scenario: Unconfigured vs. Configured Execution}

\vspace{0.8mm}
\noindent
A common misconception is that ArduPilot automatically switches off its GPS when jammed. \textbf{It does not.} The GPS module continues transmitting noisy coordinates. Understanding the internal failure sequence is critical:

\vspace{1mm}
\noindent
\footnotesize
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.18}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{L{0.48\textwidth} | Y}
\toprule
\rowcolor{aerolight}
\textbf{Default / Unconfigured Autopilot (Failure Chain)} & \textbf{Properly Configured Autopilot (Safe Execution)} \\
\midrule
\textbf{1. DCM Fallback Trap (\texttt{AHRS\_OPTIONS = 0}):} \newline
EKF3 flags innovation anomaly (\texttt{pos\_horiz\_ratio > 1.0}) and rejects GPS. However, \texttt{AP\_AHRS} sees receiver status is still $\ge \text{3D FIX}$ and automatically falls back to legacy DCM. DCM has \textbf{no dead reckoning filter}; it feeds raw jumping coordinates straight to L1 navigation, commanding erratic banks left/right (\textbf{hunting/wandering}). & 
\textbf{1. Innovation Gate Rejection (\texttt{EK3\_GLITCH\_RAD = 15}):} \newline
EKF3 checks incoming coordinates against inertial propagation. Corrupted jumps exceeding 15\,m are rejected at the gate. \\
\rowcolor{aerorow}
\textbf{2. Attitude Horizon Corruption (\texttt{AHRS\_GPS\_USE = 1}):} \newline
Centrifugal turn compensation calculates $a_{\text{cent}} = \omega_z \cdot V_{\text{gps}}$. Erratic GPS groundspeed spikes corrupt the artificial horizon, inducing violent wing-rocking. & 
\textbf{2. DCM Lockout Active (\texttt{AHRS\_OPTIONS = 3}):} \newline
Fallback to DCM is strictly inhibited (Bit 0: Plane, Bit 1: VTOL). Navigation remains locked inside EKF3 Dead Reckoning. \\
\textbf{3. Position Timeout Reset (\texttt{EK3\_OPTIONS = 0}):} \newline
After 5--10\,s of rejected GPS, EKF3's position timeout expires. It resets its position state directly onto the bad GPS coordinates! & 
\textbf{3. Horizon Isolated \& Reset Inhibited (\texttt{AHRS\_GPS\_USE 0}, \texttt{EK3\_OPTIONS 1}):} \newline
Attitude uses pitot airspeed; horizon stays level. EKF3 never resets to bad GPS; it holds dead reckoning indefinitely. \\
\rowcolor{aerorow}
\textbf{Result:} Aircraft weaves violently, loses altitude, or departs controlled flight. & 
\textbf{Result:} Smooth, straight waypoint tracking on internal aerodynamic Dead Reckoning. Zero hunting or wandering. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\noindent{\large\bfseries\color{aeroblue} 5. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Flight Protocol}

\vspace{1.5mm}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=aerolight,colframe=aeroborder,arc=1.5mm,boxrule=0.7pt,top=2.5mm,bottom=2.5mm,left=4mm,right=4mm]
\textbf{\large\color{aeroblue}THE GOLDEN RULE OF DEAD RECKONING: THE WIND-LEARNING ORBIT} \\[1mm]
{\normalsize Dead Reckoning computes ground track from the vector triangle: $\vec{V}_{\text{ground}} = \vec{V}_{\text{air}} + \vec{W}_{\text{wind}}$. \\
If an aircraft flies into a jamming zone immediately after takeoff without learning the wind, EKF3 will assume zero wind and drift with the prevailing wind. \\[1mm]
\textbf{MANDATORY SOP:} Climb to cruise altitude in good GPS and fly in \textbf{AUTO turns} or a \textbf{LOITER circle for 20--30 seconds}. This enables EKF3 to observe pitot true airspeed against groundspeed through $360^\circ$ and converge the wind vector ($\vec{W}$) to $< 0.5\text{ m/s}$ accuracy prior to ingress.}
\end{tcolorbox}
